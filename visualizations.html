01 / Family law · India

Who enters marriage after divorce becomes harder?

Following India’s restriction on husbands’ access to unilateral divorce, first-marriage entry falls among girls aged 12–17 who have not completed primary school. We do not find a statistically clear effect among girls of the same age who completed primary school.

02 / Inheritance · Pakistan

Marriage timing when inheritance lies ahead

In our study of land-record reform in Punjab, we examine marriage timing among women whose fathers were still alive or died in 2020 or later. The estimates point to later marriage in this group.

03 / Inheritance · Pakistan

Who controls inherited land?

Receiving inherited land does not necessarily mean having a say in its use. Among inheritors with usable survey responses, 37.8% of women are reported to participate in major decisions about the land, compared with 59.8% of men.
